EatWell support

Questions, problems or something that looks wrong: email nathan@fore.works. It helps to say which restaurant and item, and what you expected to see.

Common questions

What do the numbers mean?

Every figure is the restaurant's own published nutrition information unless it is marked ~estimated. An estimate is used only when a restaurant publishes nothing for that item, and it is always labelled. A total marked partly estimated includes at least one estimate.

Which restaurants are covered?

34 UK chains that publish full nutrition information, searchable in the app. For other restaurants, you can scan a screenshot of your order and EatWell will estimate it, with every number labelled.

Why does it say scanning is paused?

Scanning has a daily limit, per phone and overall, to keep the service running. Search, your basket and your history keep working, and scanning comes back the next day.

A number looks wrong

Use Report a problem with this scan on the result, which shows you exactly what will be sent before anything is. For a searched item, email us with the restaurant and the item.

How do I delete my data?

Your basket, history and recent searches are stored only on your phone: deleting the app deletes them. If you sent a problem report and want it deleted, email us and say roughly when you sent it. See the privacy policy for everything EatWell handles.
